hybrid polymer manufacturing

combined additive and subtractive manufacturing

jan 2026 ~ apr 2026


  • python
  • 3d printing
  • machining

◂
▸

i helped maintain the hybrid polymer machine in the manufacturing innovation lab, built by a former member of the lab. i replaced faulty components, troubleshooted printing and machining issues, and tested various manufacturing parameters to find optimal values for pla (polylactic acid) parts. i also implemented a helical tool path to reduce shock and wear on tooling.


i ran calibration tests with calipers to ensure equal tool engagement on all sides and measured surface roughness (ra) with a profilometer.


throughout my time working on this machine and process, i also wrote a python script to parse slicer generated gcode and automate the gcode manipulation required for the machine, reducing processing time down from a few minutes to a few seconds.
